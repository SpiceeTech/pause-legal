Pause — Delete Specific Data

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Pause (by SpiceeTech) lets you delete individual content without deleting your whole account. This page covers that — see deleting your account instead if you want everything gone.

Deleting a photo or post

  1. Open the photo in the feed or an album.
  2. Tap the menu (•••) on it.
  3. Choose Delete.

This is immediate and permanent — the photo, its file in storage, and any reactions on it are removed at once. There's no retention period.

If you can't open the app

Email mongezimthethwa56@gmail.com from the address on your account, describing what to remove (e.g. a specific photo or album). We'll verify and process the request within 30 days.

What's kept

Deleting a specific photo or post only removes that item. Your account, profile, and the rest of your vault's content are untouched.
