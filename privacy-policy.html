Pause — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Pause ("we", "us") is a private shared photo gallery app operated by SpiceeTech. This policy explains what data Pause collects, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account info: email address, username, display name, and an optional profile photo.
  • Content you share: the photos you upload to a vault, and any reactions/comments on them. Vault media is end-to-end encrypted — it's encrypted on your device before upload, and only members of that vault hold the keys to decrypt it.
  • Push notification token: if you enable notifications, a device token used to deliver them (via Expo's push service).
  • Crash and error diagnostics: if the app crashes or hits an error, basic technical details (device type, OS version, stack trace) are sent to our error-monitoring provider (Sentry) so we can fix bugs. This does not include your photos.

How it's stored

Account data and vault media are stored with Supabase (hosted in the EU). Access to a vault's content is restricted to that vault's members, enforced at the database level — not just filtered in the app. Vault media is additionally end-to-end encrypted, so even we cannot read it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the service providers that make the app work: Supabase (hosting, database, storage), Sentry (crash/error reporting), and Expo/Google (push notification delivery). Each only receives what it needs to perform its function.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently removes your profile, and — if you're the last member of a vault — that vault and everything in it. If a vault has other members, ownership passes to another member so their shared content isn't lost, and your own account and its data are still fully deleted. See how to delete your account for full details, including what to do if you can't open the app.

Children's privacy

Pause is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify users in-app.
